Why security

I started writing Java in NetBeans in Grade 10 and have been building things ever since. Security became personal when I saw how a single hijacked social media account could be turned against everyone in its contact list. One weak point was enough to reach dozens of people.

That is the idea I carry into every project: defend in layers, and make the secure path the easy path for users and developers alike.

Strong security is as much about leadership as technology. It depends on clear priorities, well-run teams and decisions that make sense to the whole business. That is why I am building my technical foundation alongside experience in leading projects and people.

Career roadmap

I’m in the early stages of my career. My long-term ambition is to grow into executive technology or information security leadership — as a Chief Information Officer (CIO) or a Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). These are two possible long-term paths, not roles I expect to step into immediately, and my specialisation will develop as I gain industry experience.

  1. Stage 1

    Build the foundation

    • Pursue internships, graduate programmes and entry-level roles in IT, software development, cybersecurity or IT support
    • Gain practical experience, strengthen my technical skills and learn from experienced professionals
    • Apply my academic knowledge to real-world challenges
  2. Stage 2

    Develop a specialisation

    • Build deeper expertise in a suitable area of technology or cybersecurity
    • Earn relevant industry certifications and pursue professional development
    • Understand how technology, risk, security and business objectives work together
  3. Stage 3

    Progress into senior roles

    • Take on greater responsibility, lead projects and mentor colleagues
    • Develop strategic decision-making skills
    • Build experience in technology operations, information security, governance, risk management and team leadership
  4. Stage 4

    Aspire to executive leadership

    • As a CIO: align technology strategy with organisational goals
    • Or as a CISO: lead information security strategy, governance and risk
    • A long-term ambition that needs sustained experience, leadership development and continuous learning

Let’s talk

I’m open to graduate roles and programmes in IT, information security and application support, where I can build a strong foundation for technology and security leadership.
