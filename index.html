Why security

I started writing Java in NetBeans in Grade 10 and have been building things ever since. Security became personal when I saw how a single hijacked social media account could be turned against everyone in its contact list. One weak point was enough to reach dozens of people.

That is the idea I carry into every project: defend in layers, and make the secure path the easy path for users and developers alike.

Strong security is as much about leadership as technology. It depends on clear priorities, well-run teams and decisions that make sense to the whole business. That is why I am building my technical foundation alongside experience in leading projects and people.

SIC Connect

Second place overall in the Samsung Innovation Campus hackathon. Built in one week and pitched to Samsung and industry experts on 24 July 2026.

An alumni system that lets everyone who has been through the Samsung Innovation Campus stay connected, find opportunities and showcase their career progress and skills.

  • Led the team as project manager and team leader, from requirements to a working full-stack build in one week

  • React and Tailwind CSS frontend, ASP.NET Core Web API in C# with Swagger documentation, Microsoft SQL Server
  • Secrets kept out of source control, with configuration supplied through environment settings
